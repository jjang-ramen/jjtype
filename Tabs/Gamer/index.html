Gaming Variable Typeface
GAMER

GAMER is a variable display typeface built around arcade screens, HUDs, and scoreboards. The VEAL axis shifts its weight and stance, from clean UI text to glitchy, aggressive letterforms.

Designer: Jang, JunYong Axis: VEAL (0–100) Format: TTF

Download

Download the current test version of GAMER and use it in your own overlays, posters, and streaming graphics.

Download Gamer.ttf

Notes

The VEAL axis shifts the internal shapes and rhythm of each glyph. Lower values feel more neutral, while higher values become louder and more distorted—perfect for kill screens and boss titles.
